## 4. numpydoc parsing of the docstring

numpydoc is what Sphinx uses to render NumPy-style docstrings.

**Sample format**

- Warnings: ['potentially wrong underline length... \nParameters: \n======================================================================= in \nParameters:\n=======================================================================...', 'potentially wrong underline length... \nReturns: \n======================================================================= in \nParameters:\n=======================================================================...', 'potentially wrong underline length... \nRaises: \n======================================================================= in \nParameters:\n=======================================================================...', 'Unknown section Parameters:', 'Unknown section Returns:', 'Unknown section Raises:']

- Exceptions recognised: none

**Standard NumPy format**

- Warnings: none
